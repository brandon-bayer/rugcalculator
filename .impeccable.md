## Design Context

### Users
Sales staff at a rug/flooring company. They use this as a utility tool to quickly calculate custom rug pricing — material costs, edge finishing, markups, trade discounts — likely on laptops or iPads in a showroom or at a desk. Speed and clarity matter most; they need to glance at results confidently.

### Brand Personality
Refined, warm, professional

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Clean utility tool with warm, sophisticated material palette. Not flashy — quietly polished.
- **Typography**: Instrument Serif for display, Plus Jakarta Sans for UI. Elegant but not decorative.
- **Color**: Warm neutrals with bronze/leather accent (#9C7B5C). Background off-white, cards white.
- **Theme**: Light and dark mode required.
- **Anti-references**: Should not look like a generic Bootstrap calculator or a consumer app. Should feel like an internal professional tool with taste.

### Design Principles
1. **Cohesive rhythm** — Consistent spacing scale and sizing proportions throughout. Every measurement should feel intentional.
2. **Scannable results** — The output side is the most important. Numbers should be immediately readable with clear visual hierarchy.
3. **Quiet confidence** — Subtle refinement over flashy effects. Transitions and shadows serve function, not decoration.
4. **Utility first** — Every element earns its space. No ornament for ornament's sake.
5. **Adaptive comfort** — Light and dark modes should both feel native, not like an afterthought.
